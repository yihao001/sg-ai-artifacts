There have been several efforts at making the contents of legal records, such as the Hansard, more easily navigable:
- [PairSearch](https://search.pair.gov.sg/?source=hansard), from OGP. More of a search engine (links you to the primary source) but also comes with LLM-generated outputs such as policy timeline, participant backgrounds and theme categorisation.
- [Open Hansard](https://openhansard.sg/), nicer palette and comes with sitting-level and participant-level profiles. [\[Github\]](https://github.com/roycehoe/hansard)
- [SGParliament](https://www.sgparliament.com/), has the deepest and most varied categorisations.
