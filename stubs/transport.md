On top of the deluge of bus/MRT apps we already have, here are some relatively novel additions in recent years with unique selling points:
- [BusRouter SG](https://busrouter.sg) - View bus routes on a map! Also has sister sites such as [RailRouter](https://railrouter.sg) with station exits marked out and [TaxiRouter](https://taxirouter.sg) with taxi stands marked out.
- [nextstopsg](https://nextstopsg.app) - 3D interactive map, you can actually watch the buses travelling in real-time (interpolated data)!
- [MRT Announcements](https://kavyaj.github.io/mrt-announcements/) - If you have children who love trains, they might like this too!
