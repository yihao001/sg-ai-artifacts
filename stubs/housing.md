Besides the litany of data science 101 projects/notebooks on HDB housing prices, here are some more useful resources:
- [housing.platform.sg](https://housing.platform.sg) - gives a nice overview of median PSF across public/private housing across all towns, along with more fine-grained project and street level information and historical trends. On top of sales transactions/yield/returns, there's rental and overall activity levels shown as well.
- [wheretobto](https://wheretobto.com) - a map-based view of past and upcoming BTOs, providing a conveninent view of amenities around the area alongside project information. (e.g. waiting time, flat types, pricing). Comes with calculators/planners. [\[Github\]](https://github.com/radiansnail-1/bto-narrowing-map)
